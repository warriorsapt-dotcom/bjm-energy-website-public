A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Before you begin.

What information helps with solar sizing?

Your electricity usage or recent bills, the appliances you want to power, when you use them, available installation space and any backup requirements help guide the sizing discussion.

Do you support both homes and businesses?

Yes. BJM Energy Solution LTD provides solar sizing, design, consultation, installation and maintenance for residential and commercial properties.

Can I get help with an existing system?

Maintenance is one of our services. Prepare a brief with your existing equipment, system age and any issues you have noticed to guide the consultation.
